What the file needs

A header row, then one row per device. The only required column is Serial number or Device name; these are also understood:

Serial number · Device name · Type · Date · Owner · IMEI 1 · IMEI 2 · IMEI 3 · Asset tag · Manufacturer · Model · Department · Section · Location · Status · Purchase cost · Warranty expiry · Notes
  • Type is matched to the registry's types (Laptop, Desktop, Printer, Phone, Tablet, Network, Server, Monitor, Other). "Mobile" becomes Phone, or Tablet for iPads and Galaxy Tabs.
  • Dates are read day-first (15/03/2024 = 15 March). Excel dates work as they are.
  • Duplicates are never overwritten. A serial number that is already registered (or repeated in the file) is skipped and listed in the preview. Placeholders like “N/A” may repeat.
  • Manufacturer is worked out from the device name when you don't give one. Up to three IMEI numbers go into their own columns exactly as written.
